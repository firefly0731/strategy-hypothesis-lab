| V1 | Carr & Wu (2009); Almeida et al. (2025, arXiv 2410.15195). Short 30d variance (DVOL vs realised) on BTC, ETH; daily delta-hedged short-gamma proxy | 2 assets × 2 hedge freqs × 4 cost haircuts = 16 | **BTC VRP real but fading.** IV − RV ≈ +5 vol pts (5m RV). NW t is 2.0 with 5m RV and 4.3 with daily-close RV; the two estimators disagree because daily closes miss intraday variance. Year by year: 2022 +0.126, 2025 +0.013, **2026 −0.008** (gone). The premium is ≈ 0 in the low-vol regime, contrary to Almeida et al.'s RND-cluster result. **ETH: no premium** (5m VRP −0.02). Short-gamma proxy, BTC, 1 vp cost: Sharpe 1.49 (5m hedge) and 1.74 (daily hedge), but skew −15, worst day −38× daily theta, MDD at 20% vol −77% (5m hedge). Earns on range days and loses on trend days (a true range strategy). Correlation with momentum −0.11: +24 bp on momentum's worst-third days, −58 bp on its best-third days.
