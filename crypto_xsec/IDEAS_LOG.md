# Cross-sectional / literature-based ideas (crypto_xsec)

| # | Source | Variants | Result | Decision |
|---|--------|----------|--------|----------|
| X1 | Heston, Korajczyk & Sadka (2010): same-time-of-day continuation (lag = 1 day) | 1 spec (18 lags × 2 estimators, descriptive) | **Not replicated.** At lag 48 (1 day) the tercile spread is −0.28 bp/half-hour (t −2.6) and FM γ is −0.10% (t −0.7), against +3.0 bp (t > 9) in the paper. Only lag 1 shows strong reversal (−1.9 bp, t −15), below maker round-trip cost. Weak weekly-lag continuation (lag 336: +0.42 bp, t 4.1) is far below costs | rejected (standalone) |
| X2 | Avellaneda & Lee (2010): residual OU stat-arb, rules as published (60d, ±1.25 / −0.50 / +0.75, τ < 30d, 5 bp) | 6 (BTC factor, PCA1, crypto cost, gross, 2 Nagel conditions) | **Not replicated.** BTC factor: gross −0.9 bp/day, net Sharpe −0.53 (t −1.2). PCA1: gross −2.0 bp/day, Sharpe −1.03. Negative in 4 of 5 years even before costs. (A first run showed Sharpe −5; that was a one-day misalignment of the P&L returns, now fixed and covered by an alignment test and a perturbation test) | rejected |
| X3 | Nagel (2012): market-adjusted daily reversal, lags 1–5; also a vol-conditioned AL | 5 + 2 | Cross-check agrees with X2: no daily cross-sectional reversal in crypto. Gross is −9 to −17 bp/day at lags 1–3 and 5 (slight continuation), with 2.7×/day turnover. Conditioning on high market vol does not rescue it | rejected |
